About Me

Hi, I'm Beecroft Arnold Oluwatobiloba, a technology and data professional passionate about applying data science, artificial intelligence, and software engineering to solve complex challenges in the energy sector, particularly the oil and gas industry.

My goal is to leverage AI and advanced analytics to optimize operations, enhance efficiency, and accelerate digital transformation across the energy industry while contributing to a more intelligent, resilient, and sustainable future.
